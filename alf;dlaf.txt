Step 2: Enable CGI in IIS
Open IIS Manager.
Click on your server in the left panel.
In the center panel, double-click "Handler Mappings".
On the right, click "Add Module Mapping...".
Set:
Request path: *
Module: FastCgiModule
Executable: Path to your Python executable, e.g.
C:\inetpub\QRcodesystem\qr_trip_project\.venv\Scripts\python.exe|wfastcgi.py
Name: Python FastCGI
Click OK and confirm if prompted.
